About This Project

A university group project — AI-Powered Academic Performance Prediction

Problem Statement

Students may struggle to understand how their current study habits and academic factors relate to their expected examination performance. Without clear, data-driven feedback, it is difficult to identify areas for improvement before it is too late.

Proposed Solution

An ML-powered web application that uses five key student habit and academic features to estimate an expected exam score. The prediction is produced by a Random Forest Regressor trained on a real dataset of student habits and performance.
